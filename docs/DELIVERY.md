## Practical limits and deferred capabilities

The semantic corpus is small and synthetic. Both models produced a wrong topical-support outcome in the held-out set; no thresholds were tuned and no calibrated accuracy or hallucination-prevention guarantee is claimed. Reading-order checks remain narrow advisories. The structural Yes applies only to the documented text profile.

Serialized report admission is not a total-RAM measurement or hard memory ceiling. The device benchmark measures a fixed workload, not a document ETA or universal computer requirement. Queue work has no persistence, reload recovery or guaranteed background execution. Large ZIP exports, durable source permissions, revised-file comparison, universal graphics/reading-order interpretation and generative repair remain future capabilities.

PDF text outside the bundled font uses a disclosed browser-raster fallback; it is nonselectable/nonextractable and depends on available browser fonts. Detailed JSON preserves exact Unicode values. Exported records describe the unchanged source PDF and do not repair or certify it.
